E_k Nakajima Quiver Varieties

The ALE gravitational instantons of affine E6, E7, E8

Let \(\widehat Q\) be the affine ADE quiver whose underlying graph is the affine Dynkin diagram of type E6, E7 or E8, with dimension vector \(v=\delta\), the coefficients of the null root, so that \(2\delta_i=\sum_{j\sim i}\delta_j\) at every node. A one-dimensional framing \(w=e_j\) at a node of mark \(1\) then forces \(\dim_{\mathbb H}M(v,w)=1\), so the Nakajima quiver variety \(M(v,w)\) is four real dimensions: the ALE gravitational instanton, the minimal resolution of \(\mathbb C^2/\Gamma\).

Deleting the affine node leaves the finite Dynkin tree, whose nodes index the exceptional \(\mathbb{P}^1\)'s of the core — six spheres for E6, seven for E7, eight for E8 — meeting at points dual to the Dynkin edges. The widget below draws this core as an externally tangent sphere packing and lets you move a marker through its normal directions with the same interaction as the hyperpolygon side view. The Flag incidence view shows three marked projective lines for the E6 chart.

Line 3 uses \(|z|\); its needle shows \(\arg z\). The table records complex incidence. The type-D4 member of the family is the hyperpolygon.
